Work register.

This register records current work in the Causal Resilience programme, which develops the Causal Governance Framework’s diagnostic foundations and approach to implementation.

Entries identify the programme, author and development status. Their inclusion does not establish publication, completed application or validation of CGF’s causal claims. The current work remains conceptual and developmental.

Causal Resilience: Upgrading Governance for the Polycrisis Age

Part I of the Causal Resilience programme. Develops the diagnostic and conceptual foundations: why tightly coupled risks expose institutional design weaknesses, how causal pathways compound, and why the preservation of room for action matters before disruption becomes systemic.

Causal Resilience II: Operationalising Governance in the Polycrisis Age

Part II of the Causal Resilience programme. Concerned with implementation: how states, regulators, firms and public institutions redesign authority, decision-making, anticipation, optionality and legitimacy under pressure, scarcity, incomplete information and political constraint.
